Exports don't say which character they came from, so add your characters above and pick one for each file. When two exports of the same type have the same character, only the newest is counted.

Permits are added up across the selected characters. Each grade column shows how many permits are held for that grade (G0 is ungraded); ∞ means unlimited. A blank cell means the item can't be crafted at that grade. Choosing grades hides items that can't be crafted at any of them, and the totals then count only those grades. Ticking several options in one filter shows items that match any of them. Category and tier come from item-catalog.js; augments or blueprints marked “not in catalog” use the export's own category until you add them there.
