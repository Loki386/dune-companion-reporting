Drop a dune-augments-*.json or blueprints export here

Or use “Load exports…”. Files are only read in this browser and remembered here. Loading a newer export of the same kind replaces the older one.

Each grade column shows how many permits you hold for that grade; ∞ means unlimited. A blank cell means the item can't be crafted at that grade. Choosing grades hides items that can't be crafted at any of them, and the totals then count only those grades. Ticking several options in one filter shows items that match any of them. Category and tier come from item-catalog.js; items marked “not in catalog” use the export's own category until you add them there.
